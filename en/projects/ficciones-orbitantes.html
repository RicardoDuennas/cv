Ficciones orbitantes

Date
November 2023
Venue
Bogotá Planetarium and Mapa Teatro's venue. Mapa Teatro lab in partnership with Plataforma Bogotá
Role
Interaction programming for full dome audiovisuals; lab participant
Technologies
openFrameworks, C++, Pure Data, NDI
Credits
Participants: Daniel Arias Mutis, Daniela Bright, Diego Felipe Gaitán Lozano, Sara Idárraga Hamid, Miguel Marañas, Mery Rodríguez, Adriana Rojas Pretel, Gerrit Stollbrock, María Constanza Tovar, Justino Velandia, Ricardo Dueñas. Mapa Teatro: Ximena Vargas, Heidy Abderhalden, Rolf Abderhalden.
Links
  • Bogotá Planetarium

An artistic experimentation lab focused on deploying Mapa Teatro's methodologies through the creation of a poetic artifact within a speculative fiction. Directed by Mapa Teatro in partnership with Plataforma Bogotá.

Over 10 sessions, the 18 participants immersed themselves in a long-range speculative fiction exercise that culminated in a poetic artifact able to link the Bogotá planetarium with Mapa Teatro's physical venue. The process produced a performative piece designed specifically for the full dome format, presented during the Festival.

Taking as its starting point the successful launch of Colombia's second satellite, the lab explored three key concepts: shelter, orphanhood and saturation. These notions organize a set of gestures and fictional stories in which image, sound and action trace our connection to the space of satellite orbit.

My contribution: interdisciplinary development of an interactive visualization and sound projection system for the full dome format over the NDI network protocol.
