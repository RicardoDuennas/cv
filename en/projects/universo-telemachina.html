Date
April 2022
Venue

Role

Technologies

Winner of the 2021 IDARTES Creation Grant, by the band Telemachina. “This is our universe: sounds that carry us to alternate realities, images that tell us stories of a parallel world. We are Telemachina, the best is yet to be discovered.”

Navigate the virtual space and trigger the sound tracks by approaching the columns of smoke. You will hear every track of the song once all the columns are lit.
