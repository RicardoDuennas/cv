Profile

I am an electronics engineer passionate about technology as a catalyst for educational interactions and a vehicle for creative experimentation. I have extensive knowledge of Unity, programming for the arts and the development of interactive environments. My experience in education and electronic arts has allowed me to collaborate on transdisciplinary projects that merge art and technology, creating unique and innovative experiences.

I focus on bringing diverse disciplines into dialogue in the service of artistic creation and arts education. I am passionate about the role technology plays in the ways we create and experience, and about the role of collectives as agents of social change.
