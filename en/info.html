Info

Ricardo Alfonzo Dueñas is a Colombian electronics engineer who uses technology as a vehicle for experimentation, connecting the body and the technological device through the creative act. A Unity developer and interaction programmer, he has collaborated on transdisciplinary projects that merge art and technology: interactive installations, virtual reality, full dome formats and robotics for the performing arts.

An early closeness to contemporary dance and a more recent, lasting one to tango keep attention to the body and movement present throughout his exploration. His experience in electronic arts education has led him to work in settings where science and technology are understood as means of sensitive creation.

He stands out for bringing diverse disciplines into dialogue in the service of artistic creation and arts education. He is passionate about the role technology plays in the ways we create and experience, and about the role of collectives as agents of social change.
